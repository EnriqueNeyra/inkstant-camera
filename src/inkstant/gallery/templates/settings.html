      Update the camera?

      The camera downloads the update, installs it and restarts. This takes about 5 to 10 minutes. Keep it charging, or above 30%. The camera's buttons pause until it's done; pressing power turns it off once the update finishes. This page stops working while it restarts.

      Turn on developer mode?

      The camera will join your home Wi-Fi every time it starts, so you can reach it over SSH. This uses more battery.

      What the light means

      • Off The camera is off. Also off during a slideshow wake. At the end of a shutdown, it's safe to pull the module.
      • Solid Ready to shoot.
      • Slow blink Wait: starting up, developing a photo, waking to take one, turning off, or updating.
      • A short wink The shutter press was accepted.
      • Long on, short off Sharing is on.
      • A short flash every 4 s Asleep: press the shutter to shoot.
      • Three quick blinks A problem; this page explains it.

      Played once, then back to the above

      • Fast blink for 2 s No display module.
      • Three double blinks Battery low.
